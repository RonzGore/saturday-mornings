Privacy

Effective 25 October 2026

The short version. Saturday Mornings lets parents give each kid a TV channel of YouTube shows that comes on at set times and signs off on its own. We keep as little as we can, we never sell it, we show no ads and use no tracking cookies, and kids never sign in or give us anything. You can delete everything yourself, at any time.

2. Who this covers

This policy covers our website (saturdaymornings.tv), the parent app and sign-in (app.saturdaymornings.tv, including the app you add to your phone's home screen), and our TV apps for Samsung, Android TV and Google TV. It covers parents who sign in, the kids who watch, and anyone who joins our waiting list.

3. What we keep

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes and to know which household is yours.
  • Your picture, if you add one, which is a small square copy of the photo you choose (made on your phone) or the emoji you pick. Only the grown-ups in your family see it; kids never do. Choose “Use my initials” to remove it.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on. We keep the address your phone's or browser's push service gives us for sending them (Apple, Google or Mozilla delivers them, encrypted so they can't read them), and which alerts you want. Turn them off in Account and the address is deleted.
  • Passkeys, if you add one. We store only the public key your device creates. Your fingerprint, face or device PIN never leaves your device.
  • What you set up for your kids, which is the display name you type for each kid (a nickname is fine), the emoji and colour you pick, their channels and times, and the shows you choose (YouTube video, channel and playlist IDs, with their titles and thumbnail images).
  • Your linked TVs, meaning the name you give each TV and when it last checked for changes. A TV holds its own link key; we keep only a scrambled copy to check it.
  • Your family, meaning the email address of any parent you invite, and which parents share your family. Everyone in a family sees and can change the same kids, shows, times and TVs. The family's time zone comes from the first phone that opens it.
  • Abuse protection. To stop people guessing codes or flooding inboxes, we count recent requests by email address and by IP address.
  • Error logs from our hosting provider, which can include IP addresses and the pages requested, plus error reports the app and TV send when something breaks (the error message and the browser or TV model).

4. What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • We don't record what your kids watch. The TV remembers where it got to in a lineup on the TV itself.
  • Kids never create accounts, sign in or type anything in. Only a parent can set up a household.
  • The TV apps have no analytics and no advertising code in them.

Visitor counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to our website and the parent app (not the TV apps). It records which pages are opened, roughly where from (the country), and what kind of device and browser. It uses no cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't identify you. It never sees what's in your account, your kids' channels or what they watch.

Bot protection. To keep bots away from sign-in and the waiting list, the "Email me a code" form, the coupon and the TV petition use Cloudflare Turnstile. It usually works invisibly. It looks at signals from your browser and connection, such as your IP address and the type of browser and device, to tell a person from a bot, and only asks you to tick a box when it isn't sure. Cloudflare uses this information to run the check and keep the service secure, not for advertising. Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use apply.

5. The waiting list

If you leave your email on saturdaymornings.tv to reserve a channel or sign the TV petition (signing the petition also puts you on the waiting list), or try to sign in to the app before it's open to you, we keep your email address, the TVs you picked, the country your request came from (worked out by Cloudflare; we don't store your IP address) and when you signed up. We use it to send you one thank-you email straight away, to tell you when your channel or your TV is ready (that email offers your first month free), and to see which TVs families want next. Nothing else, and we never share it. To come off the waiting list, reply to any of our emails or write to CONTACT_EMAIL, and we'll delete your entry.

6. YouTube

Saturday Mornings uses YouTube API Services. Videos play in YouTube's own player and come straight from YouTube, and when a video plays, YouTube (Google) may collect information under the Google Privacy Policy. By using Saturday Mornings you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube may show its own ads in some videos, as it does in its own apps; we have no control over those and earn nothing from them. To show your show library, we load YouTube thumbnail images through our own server, so YouTube doesn't see your phone or TV fetching each one. We keep YouTube titles and thumbnails for at most 30 days, refreshing them while you use them, as YouTube's developer policies require.

7. Who helps us run it

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, the apps and the database (with its backups and short-lived logs), counts visits (Web Analytics) and runs the bot checks (Turnstile).
  • Resend sends our sign-in codes, family invites and waiting-list emails.
  • YouTube (Google) plays the videos and provides their titles and thumbnails.
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla deliver notifications, if you turn them on, to the phones and browsers they make.

They only get what they need to do that job. Some of them process information outside Canada, mainly in the United States, where it may be accessible to courts and authorities under local law.

8. Cookies and storage

We use one cookie to keep you signed in, and one that lasts 10 minutes while you enter a sign-in code. A linked TV keeps its link, and where it got to in each lineup, in the TV app's own storage. That's all.

9. How long we keep things

  • Your account, kids' channels, library, family and TVs stay while you have an account. Delete it any time (see below).
  • Sign-in codes last 10 minutes, and signed-in sessions up to 60 days or until you sign out.
  • Family invites that aren't used expire after 14 days.
  • Abuse-protection counters last under 24 hours, and notification records (so an alert isn't sent twice) 3 days.
  • Error logs are kept for a few days.
  • YouTube titles and thumbnails are kept for at most 30 days, refreshed while in use.
  • The waiting list keeps your entry until you're let in, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • Backups kept by our database provider expire within 30 days.

10. Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. There are no passwords to steal, because you sign in with an emailed code or a passkey, codes are stored only in scrambled form, and every request is checked against your own family. See how we keep Saturday Mornings safe, and how to report a problem.

11. Your choices and rights

You can see and change everything we keep in the app at any time. To delete everything, open Account › Delete account. Your account, your kids' channels, your library and every linked TV are deleted straight away. If another parent shares your family, only your account is deleted and the family stays with them. Copies in backups expire within 30 days.

To ask for a copy of your information, to correct something, or with any privacy question or complaint, email CONTACT_EMAIL. We answer within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the privacy regulator where you live. In British Columbia that's the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC, and elsewhere in Canada the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

12. Children

Saturday Mornings is a tool for parents, made for families with young children. Kids watch the channels on the TV, but they never create accounts, sign in, type anything in or give us information, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The only things about a child we hold are what a parent types in (a display name, an emoji and a colour), and parents can change or delete them at any time. The TV apps show only the shows a parent chose, have no ads, analytics or links out to other apps or websites, and can't be used to buy anything. If you believe a child has given us information, tell us at CONTACT_EMAIL and we'll delete it.
